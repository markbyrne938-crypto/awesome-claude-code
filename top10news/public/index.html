    How stories are ranked

    Every couple of minutes we read the headline feeds of Reuters, Associated Press, AFP, BBC News, The New York Times, The Washington Post, The Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, Deutsche Welle and France 24. Headlines about the same event are grouped together. A story's score is the sum, across every source covering it, of that source's weight times how high it places the story (wire services count most). Stories reported by several independent sources outrank single-source items, and older stories gradually fade.

    Each card shows the wire service's wording where available and links to every outlet's own report, so you can compare coverage directly. We show only headlines, short feed summaries and links.
